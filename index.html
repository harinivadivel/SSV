About Our Shop

Bringing Pure, Authentic Groceries to Every Kitchen

Shri Samuthira Vilas Maligai and General was established to provide the community of Panamangalam, Koothur, and neighboring Tiruchirappalli localities with honest weight, high quality, and fairly priced food grains and household necessities.

Strict Quality Selection

We source grains and spices directly from reliable regional millers and farmers, ensuring stones, dust, and impurities are eliminated.

Accurate Weighing & Transparent Rates

Every gram counts. We believe in 100% truthful weights, competitive retail & wholesale pack pricing without hidden markups.

Local Neighborhood Relationship

Conveniently located right by SVM School Road in Panamangalam, known for courteous service and personal attention to regular families.
